Kael’s Tavern stands on the harbor of Sea Lō — a rough, rugged seaport town of foul-smelling docks and mob connections, a dingy little port where contraband, secrets, and information are held and passed in the dark. Inside, it’s real cee-lo, your gold against Kael the Iron Mask and his purse. Win rounds and your streak pays bonus gold — but every loss Kael takes fills his temper. Fill it, and he flips the table: a Tactical Table altercation for the whole pot. Lose a round yourself, and you choose — roll again, or call him a cheat and start the fight on your terms.

    How a round scores — the roll rankings
    1. 4-5-6 — instant win, the best throw there is.
    2. Trips (all three dice the same) — beats any point. Higher trips beat lower trips; 6-6-6 tops them all, short of 4-5-6.
    3. Pair + a single — your point is the single die: pair of 3s with a 4 is point 4. Point 6 is a fortress; point 1 is a nightmare.
    4. 1-2-3 — instant loss, the cruelest throw in the game.
    5. Anything else (no pair, no run) — no score. Throw again.

    Banker’s instant settle: Kael is the banker and throws first. If he rolls 4-5-6, the bank wins the round outright — a 4-5-6 you roll after that does not overturn it. If he rolls 1-2-3, the bank loses outright and you win the round.

    Equal scores push and your bet comes back. In the altercation: Kill Box beats Feint & Flank, Shield Wall beats Kill Box, Feint & Flank beats Shield Wall, and Overturn the Bar is a coin-flip with splinters. Winning a brawl Kael started pays his whole purse; winning one you started pays back everything you’ve lost at his table, plus 30. Losing one you started gets you tossed out into the street. And cleaned out isn’t always the end: on the Cleaned out screen you can call him a cheat and fight on those same terms — win and you’re back at the table with the purse, lose and you’re tossed out.
